# 3rd Street Technologies

**Reimagining social media for a real-world-first experience.**

Staying connected online is a start, but it's not enough. Humans need real-world experiences and face-to-face interactions. We're developing apps that turn online connectedness to real-world togetherness.

- Individuals and families can discover and plan in-person activities.
- Organizers can use the apps to promote and grow local events.

Apps are in prototype stage. Follow for updates as previews are made available.
